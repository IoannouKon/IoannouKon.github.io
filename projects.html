Independent hardware project

LUMIXED ↗

A configurable Chisel RTL generator for a multiplier-free, LUT-based GEMM accelerator integrated with RocketChip through RoCC and TileLink DMA. FP16 activations produce FP32 outputs; signed INT2/INT4/INT8 weights are runtime-selectable.
